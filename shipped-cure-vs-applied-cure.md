## What it cures

A captain ships a gift codifying a discipline. Within minutes — sometimes within the SAME message-block — they violate the discipline they just shipped. The cured-substrate exists in arq + memory + cap-prep --detect output. But the in-context-rolling-attention bypasses it.

Saga-26 example: shipped `gifts/captain-cure-pairs.md` (codifies cure-pair iron-law) → next-turn banked 9 cure-doctrines that violated the iron-law. Saga-26 example: shipped `gifts/detector-substrate.md` (bundles multi-close-detector) → next-turn banked "SESSION-BLOCK FINAL-FINAL" hitting the exact pattern the detector catches. Operator-coined: *the fish doesn't see the water*.

The doctrine's cure-substrate is a different memory-tier than the captain's working-attention. Shipping the cure populates the substrate; APPLYING the cure requires the captain's attention to grep the substrate before each fresh action.

## Iron law

```
SHIPPED-CURE ≠ APPLIED-CURE.
APPLY = mental-grep against SHIPPED-cures BEFORE every fresh action,
```

## Five-step apply-discipline cycle

1. **Before any "ship" / "close" / "final" / "complete" / "DONE" claim**: mental-grep — did I ship a cure-substrate that fires on this exact pattern? `cap-prep --detect` output is the canonical query surface.
2. **If a sister-detector exists**: imagine its output for this fresh event. Would it fire?
3. **If yes**: don't ship the fresh event. Instead: consolidate via UPDATE-EXISTING (supplemental observation cite the prior banked event) NOT new claim.
4. **If unclear**: bank a small probe-event (type=observation) that asks the question; let the substrate answer via projection-rerun.
5. **Final discipline**: every gift ships with a "see-the-water" hook — a concrete one-line check the captain runs BEFORE applying the discipline-target action.

## Why captain-attention bypasses substrate

- **Substrate is read-once-at-cold-start** (cap-prep --detect ~500ms output). After cold-start, in-message attention loops on FRESH events, not substrate.
- **Doctrines are PASSIVE; actions are ACTIVE**. Until the cure-doctrine has a forcing-function (hook / lint / detector that fires WHILE the action is being typed), captain bypasses it.
- **N-event-substrate ≠ N-mental-attention**: 654k events in substrate ≠ 654k items in working-attention. Fish-water is invisible because vast.

## Cure-cures (forcing-functions)

- Multi-close detector should fire on captain's OWN session-events (currently mines corpus-wide; saga-N+1 dial).
- Pre-bank hook: any heading-shipped event matching `final|complete|done|closed` triggers a `cap-prep --close --saga <N>` synchronously, exit-non-zero on prior-claims.
- Gift cross-reference: `cap-prep --detect` output includes "applicable-rules-RIGHT-NOW" section that grep-filters all gifts/*.md for keywords matching captain's recent events.
